# Matter and Tiers

Every collector, relay and power flower comes in sixteen tiers, and the tier is named after the
matter it is built from.

The first three are borrowed rather than new:

- **Basic** is built from Diamond Blocks.
- **Dark Matter** and **Red Matter** use ProjectE's own matter.

From there the mod adds its own ladder, each one crafted from the one below it:

Magenta, Pink, Purple, Violet, Blue, Cyan, Green, Lime, Yellow, Orange, White, Fading.

The last tier is not a matter at all. **The Final Collector**, **The Final Relay** and **The Final
Power Flower** are built from the <ItemLink id="projectex:final_star_shard" />, and they are the
end of the ladder: a trillion EMC per second, and a relay with no transfer limit.

## The shape of the progression

Each tier of collector produces four times the one below it, and costs a tier of matter that was
itself made from the previous one. The cost grows as fast as the output does, so moving up a tier
is never free — but a tier you have already built keeps paying while you work towards the next.

This is why [power flowers](power-flowers.md) matter: at the top tiers the difference between
eighteen collectors and one is the difference between a build you can place and one you cannot.
