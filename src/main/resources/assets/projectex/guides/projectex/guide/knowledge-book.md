# Knowledge Sharing Book

<RecipeFor id="projectex:knowledge_sharing_book" />

Sneak and right click to sign it with your name. Anyone else who right clicks it then learns
everything you know, all at once, and the book is used up doing it.

You cannot read your own book, and you do not have to be online for someone to read yours: it goes
looking in your save file, so a book left in a chest is still worth what you knew when you signed
it. Signing it again later brings it up to date.

It teaches the same way everything else in this mod does, so anything a pack has decided cannot be
learned stays unlearned.
