# Arcane Transmutation Tablet

<RecipeFor id="projectex:arcane_tablet" />

Right click it anywhere and you get a crafting grid and a transmutation table in the same screen,
with no block placed and nothing to walk back to.

## The grid pays for itself

Take something out of the result slot and every ingredient that vanished is bought back out of your
EMC and put where it was, so you can craft the same thing again immediately. It only buys what you
already know and only while you can afford it; when either runs out the grid empties the way any
crafting table does.

Shift clicking the result crafts as many as the grid holds without buying anything back, so it is
the way to spend what is in the grid rather than what is in your balance.

The grid belongs to the tablet, not to you. Two tablets hold two grids, and a tablet you give away
arrives with whatever was left in it.

### The four small buttons

Down the left of the grid:

- **Rotate** turns the eight outer slots one step clockwise, or anticlockwise with shift held. The
  middle slot stays where it is.
- **Balance** levels every group of identical stacks. With shift held it **spreads** instead,
  filling the empty slots from the fullest ones first.
- **Search Type** decides whether the search bar takes your typing the moment the screen opens. The
  two JEI options behave like the two plain ones until JEI support lands.
- **Clear** empties the grid. Anything the table would buy back at full price is sold; everything
  else goes to your inventory, so tidying up never costs you covalence loss.

## The ring

The circle of items is everything you know, most valuable first, twelve at a time. The small number
under each one is how many of it your balance would buy right now, and it counts down as you spend.

- **Click** an item to take one onto the cursor, **shift click** for as many as fit in a stack.
- The slot in the middle of the ring **sells** whatever you are holding, and teaches it on the way
  through. With shift held it fills an empty Klein Star from your balance instead, or empties a full
  one back into it.
- **Learn** and **Unlearn** at the bottom corners do exactly that to what you are holding, without
  consuming it.
- **Shift clicking** anything in your inventory sells it, the same as it would in a transmutation
  table.

Type in the bar to search by name, or start with `@` to search by mod. Scroll anywhere in the screen
to turn the page. Right clicking the bar empties it, and clicking it while holding something
searches for that item.

What you can learn and what you can afford are both decided by the server, so the ring showing an
item is never the last word on whether you get it.
