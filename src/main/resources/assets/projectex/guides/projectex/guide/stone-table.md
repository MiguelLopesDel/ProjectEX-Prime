# Stone Transmutation Table

A Transmutation Table that can be placed on a wall or a ceiling as well as on the floor. Right
click it and you get the ordinary ProjectE transmutation interface, with your own knowledge and
your own EMC.

<RecipeFor id="projectex:stone_table" />

There is nothing else to it. It is the same table, freed from having to sit on the ground, which
is mostly a convenience when you want one at eye level next to a wall of
[links](emc-links.md).
